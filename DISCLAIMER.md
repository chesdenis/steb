# Educational use notice and disclaimer

STEB (*Ship Theory Electronic Book*) is a **teaching tool**. It was written in 2009–2010 as a diploma project and is published
for anyone interested in ship theory (floating position, hydrostatics, stability) to study,
run, extend and fork it.

## Intended use

* Learning and teaching naval architecture calculations: coursework, exercises, self-study, research prototypes.
* Experimenting with the code: forks and modifications are welcome under the terms of the [MIT License](LICENSE).

## Not intended for

* Design, approval, certification or operation of real ships, boats or floating structures.
* Loading, stability or damage-stability decisions for vessels in service.
* Submission to classification societies, flag states, port authorities or any other regulatory body.
* Any governmental, military, commercial or safety-critical use where results are relied upon.

The program has **not** been verified against any classification society rules or national/international
regulations (e.g. IMO stability criteria) and is not type-approved loading or stability software.
The included example projects use approximated, illustrative data, not real ships.
